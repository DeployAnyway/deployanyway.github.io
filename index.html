DeployAnyway.

Software nobody requested, built with questionable priorities, and shipped with absolute confidence!

Tools for developers who probably should know better. We take the jokes seriously—and the useful parts even more seriously.

small open-source tools for expressing yourself, debugging, regrouping, logging and deciding what to ship. Choose the one(s) that helps your day.

Serious about the work. Playful about the day.

Dallas and Benji, our fun-loving, high-energy huskies, are the inspiration: bring enthusiasm, make room for play, and approach the next adventure with ridiculous confidence.

Developers love to have fun, too. A debugging session can use a rubber duck, a release can use a reality check, and a terminal can use a little personality. DeployAnyway brings that energy to small tools with useful behavior and original humor. The jokes are optional; caring about the work is the point.

bro-say

Turn your message into original character art with speech or thought bubbles, personalities and themes. 


            

            
            
Try it in your terminal (sh/bash):

            

            
          

        

        

          13 original characters, 20 moods, 6 themes and 48 message presets
          across 8 categories. Say or think; Unicode-aware wrapping. Terminal
          fonts may disagree on emoji width.
        

        
Runs entirely in your browser. No message is sent anywhere.

        

          npm install -g @deployanyway/bro-say@0.4.0 ·
          All five packages on npm

doggo-log

        

          Write readable or structured logs with levels, prefixes and context
          for following a request.
        

        
        

          All six log methods are available. JSON shows request context; text
          shows readable lines. Explore 48 commentary lines across six levels,
          classic or rotating barks, and an eight-log sequence. This browser
          demo formats string messages; Node also supports format arguments,
          child loggers and ANSI color.

ship-it-meter

        

          Load any of 12 evidence scenarios, edit the facts and see prioritized
          release plans, gates or scores. A joke score is no substitute for
          tests.
        

        
        

          Change the evidence to see why a gate passes or blocks. Missing
          evidence can be explored by editing the JSON. This is a heuristic, not
          deployment approval.
