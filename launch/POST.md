DeployAnyway 1.0: fetch the request, follow the cause, bring receipts.

Dallas and Benji, our playful, high-energy huskies, remind us why we started making things: curiosity, passion, creativity and the joy of trying an interesting idea. DeployAnyway carries that spirit into five small developer tools, with useful behavior and a little mischief.

Meet the huskies and the purpose behind the project: https://deployanyway.github.io/story/

Then see the tools work together: request-scoped logs with redaction, Error/cause diagnostics, honest incident drafts, readable status summaries, and release checks backed by real tests, coverage and builds.

Try the walkthrough and copy the runnable example: https://deployanyway.github.io/launch/

It includes an actual missing-file failure and a deliberately broken build. Confidence gets a tail wag; passing commands get the release.

Software nobody requested, built with questionable priorities, and shipped with absolute confidence!
